About me

Who I am

I’m a senior high-school student in Lithuania who enjoys studying topics that feels useful or that sparks my curiosity. I enjoy understanding how things work, whether it’s technology, science, or anything that catches my interest.

What I explore

My current interests sit between computer science, engineering and science. I’m especially drawn to graphics, game development, low-level systems, and future areas like embedded technology, neuroscience and brain-machine interaction.

Where I’m heading

For now, I’m focused on building a strong foundation in computer science while continuing to develop a voxel graphics engine. Long term, I hope to explore R&D at the intersection of computing and the brain.
